# Book Recipes — Outline

How several extensions combine in one kind of book, for authors who have made a book (Your First Book) and want to make a particular kind. Each recipe is the reader's companion to an agent recipe in `docs/agent-recipes/` (`process/AGENT_RECIPES.md`): the reasons, worked examples and illustrations, not the recipe line for line. When a convention changes, update both in the same commit. Begun 2026-11-24.

Each `##` section is one chapter, `Text/<slug>.dj`, its `#` heading matching the section title. Djot, one line per paragraph; controls are `[Label]{.ui}`. Screenshots come from the invented McInnes family of Carlton (real, unidentified sitters in the photographs, from LACMA and the Rijksmuseum) in `src/stories/manual-shots/family-history-book.ts`, captured as the editor and preview side by side.

## Family history books

`family-history-books` — person chapters with records; the family panel; dates; people without a chapter; trees; lifelines; photographs with named faces; portraits; the index of people; research notes and footnotes; problems in the records.
